Lights Out

How to Play Lights Out

Lights Out is a puzzle game where you need to turn off all the lights on the grid.

Rules:

  • Click on a light to toggle it and its adjacent lights (up, down, left, right)
  • The goal is to turn off all lights (make them all dark)
  • Each level has a different starting configuration
  • Complete all levels to win the game!

Tips:

  • Think carefully about each move - it affects multiple lights
  • Some levels may require you to think several moves ahead
  • Use the Reset button if you get stuck
