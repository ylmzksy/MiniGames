How to Play Nim

Nim is an ancient game of strategy where two players take turns removing objects from piles.

Rules:

  • Click on a pile to select it
  • Choose how many sticks to remove from that pile
  • The player who takes the last stick(s) wins
  • You play against the AI opponent

Tips:

  • The game has strategic positions that can guarantee a win
  • Try to leave your opponent with equal sticks in each pile
  • When playing against the Master, strategy is crucial
